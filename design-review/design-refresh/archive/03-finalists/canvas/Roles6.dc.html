Three voices, three typefaces

TYPE ROLES

Rule: the brand is the house, the poem is the guest. The house is geometric and quiet; the guest arrives in handwriting. Never set the brand in Ruq'ah inside the app.

THE HOUSE

The app speaks

Reem Kufi · geometric, built, quiet

شعر بالعربي

Brand, navigation, settings, onboarding. Built on the same grid as the marks.

THE POEM

The poet speaks

Aref Ruqaa · handwritten, heavy, gold

أمرُّ على الديار

Poem titles only. The one place gold handwriting appears, so it always means 'a poem starts here'.

THE VERSE

The words

Amiri · Naskh with full vowels

وَما حُبُّ الدِيارِ شَغَفنَ قَلبي

Verse lines. Readable, classical, carries the vowel marks.
