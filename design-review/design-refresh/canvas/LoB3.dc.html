THE MEANING

أمرُّ على الديار

قيس بن الملوّح · Majnun Layla

He walks past the empty houses and kisses one wall, then the next. Then the turn: it was never the walls. Every brick he touches is a way of touching her, and the poem only admits it in the last half-line.

Poets before him stopped at ruined camps to weep. Majnun keeps walking.
