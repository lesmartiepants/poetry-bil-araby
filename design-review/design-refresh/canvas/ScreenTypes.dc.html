أمرُّ على الديار

I PASS BY THE DWELLINGS

قيس بن الملوّح · Majnun Layla

أَمُرُّ عَلى الدِيارِ دِيارِ لَيلى

I pass by the dwellings, Layla's dwellings,

أُقَبِّلُ ذا الجِدارَ وَذا الجِدارا

and kiss this wall, and then that wall.

وَما حُبُّ الدِيارِ شَغَفنَ قَلبي

It is not love of the dwellings that took my heart,

وَلَكِن حُبُّ مَن سَكَنَ الدِيارا

but love of the one who lived in them.

He walks past the empty houses and kisses one wall, then the next. Then the turn: it was never the walls. Every brick he touches is a way of touching her.

Poets before him stopped at ruined camps to weep. Majnun keeps walking.
