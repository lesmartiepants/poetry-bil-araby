Alhambra marks · eight geometries

ALHAMBRA · 01

01 · PAJARITA

NASRID TILES

A curved triangle the tilers repeated endlessly. Its local name means little bird; Escher studied these tilings.

02 · HUESO NAZARÍ

NASRID TILES

The interlocking Nasrid bone: every bump fits a neighbour's notch, like rhyme answering rhyme.

03 · LAZO OF 16

PALACE DADOS

A 16-point interlaced strapwork star, one of the wheels that turn through the tile dados.

04 · SEBKA

CARVED PLASTER

Overlapping pointed arches that net into diamonds, carved across the palace walls.

05 · LOBED ARCH

ARCADES

A scalloped arch set inside its rectangular frame (alfiz), the classic Nasrid doorway.

06 · SEVEN HEAVENS

HALL OF AMBASSADORS

Nested stars after the cedar ceiling of the throne hall, read as the layered heavens.

07 · ALICATADO

CUT-TILE MOSAIC

Stars cut from glazed tile and set edge to edge. The tile is the grid; the star emerges.

08 · POEM BAND

INSCRIPTIONS

The Alhambra is covered in verse by Ibn Zamrak. A text band with its medallion.
