ORNAMENTS

Pattern fades in from the top edge and never sits behind verse text.

VOICE

The verse speaks first.

The interface whispers. No badges, counters or alerts on top of a poem.

Translate the feeling, not only the words.

Yes: “It is not love of the dwellings that took my heart.”

No: “Love of houses did not affect my heart.”

Explain like a friend in the majlis, not a textbook.

Yes: “Majnun takes an old convention and makes it an obsession.”

No: “This poem utilizes the atlal motif.”

Never rush the reader.

Yes: “That's today's seven. More tomorrow.”

No: “Don't lose your streak!”
