Design language and specs

S6 · RULES FOR BUILDING

The numbers behind the components: spacing, shape, type, colour, motion, the share card and accessibility.

LAYOUT

Screen390 × 844 design frame; content in a single column.
Side margin22px everywhere; carousels bleed off the right edge.
Vertical rhythm12px between items in a group, 22–28px between sections, a kicker opens each section.
Touch targets44px minimum; primary buttons 50–54px.
Bottom clearanceContent stops 110px above the bottom for the tab bar or dock.

TYPE SCALE

Page titleCormorant 30 + Reem Kufi 26 for its Arabic.
Poem titleAref Ruqaa 700, 24–34 by context. Ruq'ah only ever for poem titles.
VerseAmiri 19–23 with full vowels; English in Cormorant italic at about two-thirds.
Body and UIJosefin Sans 13–16.
Kicker and metaJosefin 10–11, caps, 0.14–0.24em tracking.
NumbersArabic-Indic beside Arabic text, Latin beside English.

SHAPE

RadiiPhone 44 · sheets 28 · cards 14–18 · tiles 12 · pills fully round · avatars circle.
Lines1px hairlines; borders #2A2722 on dark, rules in old gold #8A7442 on cards.
ElevationOnly floating things cast a shadow: tab bar, dock, toast (0 12 32 rgba(0,0,0,.5)). Cards stay flat.

COLOUR IN USE

GoldOnly one gold primary per screen; gold also marks the active tab, chip and switch.
Era coloursPre-Islamic pomegranate · Umayyad and Andalusia verdigris · Abbasid indigo · Modern cobalt. Patterns and avatars follow them.
KeptThe pomegranate-light heart #C4566B is the only red in the interface.
Share colourwaysGold foil on obsidian · ink on parchment · gold on pomegranate · ivory on cobalt · ivory on verdigris · gold on indigo.

MOTION

PaceCalm: 200ms for taps, 280ms ease-out for sheets, 400ms for the hero folding into its band.
The markFolds the reading dock into the mark on scroll; fans the five actions out from it; stamps a kept poem.
Recipient lineTypes, holds, erases about every 2.6s.
Reduced motionEverything still: no typing, no fan, sheets cut instead of slide.

SHARE CARD

Size1080 × 1350 (4:5); link and brand in a reserved strip above the frame.
CapacityUp to 6 Arabic–English pairs a card; no limit on lines chosen.
SplittingNever split a verse; divide as evenly as the verses allow, the first card takes any extra (7 → 4 + 3, 8 → 4 + 4, 10 → 6 + 4).
Every cardIdentical header; only the number and the progress hairline change.
ScalingVerse size and the diamond separators scale together with the number of pairs.

ACCESSIBILITY

ContrastIvory on Midnight 17.1:1; old gold only for lines, never text.
LanguageArabic text tagged lang=ar and dir=rtl; English and Arabic never mirrored, each aligned to its own reading edge.
LabelsEvery icon button has a spoken label; status marks (READ, heart) are labelled too.
