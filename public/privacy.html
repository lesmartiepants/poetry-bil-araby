Privacy

Poetry Bil-Araby is designed for reading. You can explore without creating an account.

When you sign in

Google provides the basic profile information shown during sign-in: your name, email address, and profile image. Authentication is handled by Supabase. The app does not receive your Google password.

What the app stores

If you use an account, Poetry Bil-Araby may store your saved poems, reading preferences, and app settings so they follow you across devices. Essential session information is stored in your browser to keep you signed in.

Operations and reliability

The app uses service providers for hosting, authentication, product analytics, and error monitoring. Technical events help maintain reliability; authentication credentials and OAuth tokens must not be written to application logs.

Your choices

  • Read without an account.
  • Sign out at any time from the account menu.
  • Clear local browser data through your browser settings.
  • Request help with account data through the support link below.
