PATTERN LIBRARY · مكتبة الزخارف

Every poem comes from a place.
So does its pattern.

These are the patterns the app uses on its large panels: poem heroes, poet pages, collection and era cards, share cards. Each is drawn from the tile, stucco, brick, textile or metalwork of a real place in the world Arabic poetry was written in, from Granada to Sana'a.

A poem from Andalusia can sit on Alhambra lacería, an Abbasid poem on Baghdad brick, a Yemeni poet on Sana'a gypsum. The pattern quietly says where the words were born.

HOW TO READ A FRAME

AN2 · Lacería · Alhambra
Region code and number, the pattern's name, and where it comes from.

Inside each frame: the pattern large, its Arabic place name, a line on its history, three colourways from the brand palette, and where in the app it suits.

In use marks the two already in the flows (Pajarita, Khatam). Everything else is ready to swap in.

RULES OF USE

  • One pattern per panel, never two side by side on a screen.
  • The pattern takes the era colour: desert pomegranate, Abbasid indigo, Andalusia verdigris, modern cobalt.
  • Medallions (Zellij rosette, Shamseh) only on hero panels; repeating patterns on cards and tiles.
  • Text sits on a calm area or a scrim, never over the densest part.
