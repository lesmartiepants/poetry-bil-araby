# Women Who Wrote the Canon · نساء كتبن الديوان

**What have women poets made possible in Arabic verse?** Build a twelve-poem shelf from the mock’s starting points: Al-Khansa, Layla al-Akhyaliyya, Rabiʿa al-ʿAdawiyya, ʿUlayya bint al-Mahdi, and Wallada. Carry it into modernism with Nazik al-Malaika, into Palestinian poetry with Fadwa Tuqan, and into the present with Mersal.

Build around one pairing: Al-Khansa’s elegy and Tuqan’s **“I Will Not Cry” · لن أبكي**. Tuqan draws on the classical language of ruins to write about Palestinian loss. A reader who has met Al-Khansa and Imruʾ al-Qays can hear what Tuqan inherits and what she changes. [Close reading](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/13676/files/Palestine-Futurity-and-the-Rith%C4%81%CA%BE.pdf)
