HOW THE LIBRARY OF POEMS IS ORGANISED

Poemقصيدة

The unit of everything. Known by its opening line; a Ruq'ah title where tradition gives one.

Poetشاعر

Every poem has one. Named in Arabic with a plain transliteration.

Journeyرحلة

An ordered series, read one step at a time. Any length. Each step is one poem.

Collectionمجموعة

A curated set read in order or any order, with progress.

Eraعصر

Where a poem sits in time. Five eras, each with its own colour.

Feelingشعور

What a poem does to you: Longing, Grief, Pride, The divine…

Journeys and collections are made by the guide. Eras and feelings are ways to browse. Your diwan is what you keep.

BILINGUAL NAMING

Every name in both languages: titles, poets, journeys, collections, eras and feelings.

Each on its own edge: Arabic right-aligned, English left, never mirrored.

Arabic leads in poetry: poem titles and verses; English leads in the interface.

Transliteration is plain: Al-Khansa, Imru' al-Qais, Majnun Layla; no academic diacritics in the interface.

Numbers follow the language: ٣ beside Arabic, 3 beside English.

Step names are short and singular: one word that says what each step is (a heaven, a home, a woman, a voice). Shown inside the journey only, never in a journey's subtitle.

Subtitles describe the arc: "The desert camp to Palestine", not "Home 1 to Home 5".

Milestones are general: a step complete reads "Heaven three, grief." and offers Read; the end reads All [count], complete.
