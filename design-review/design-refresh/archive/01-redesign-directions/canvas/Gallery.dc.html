DIRECTION 04 · THE MUSEUM CURATOR

Gallery

Each day, one poem as an exhibition in four rooms: context, poem, voice, exit. No feed, no badges, no infinite scroll. Scarcity is the luxury.

Built from

The curator: "You're giving the poet a notification count." Plus the critique: context belongs before the poem, not hidden behind Insights.

Principles
  • One poem a day. The archive stays open.
  • Linear rooms with a single "next" door.
  • Wall-label typography: small caps, brass.
  • Sharing is the gift shop, at the exit.
Flow
  1. Entrance: today's exhibition
  2. Room I: context wall
  3. Room II: the poem
  4. Room III: the listening room
  5. Room IV: exit and gift shop
  6. Closed until tomorrow, archive open
Measure (hypothesis): returning on consecutive days and finishing all four rooms, rather than time in app.
