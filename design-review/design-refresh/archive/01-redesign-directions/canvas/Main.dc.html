DIRECTION 01 · THE UX FIX

Verse

Keep the brand, fix the product. Same night-and-gold mood, but every control earns its place and the poem becomes the thing you touch.

BUILT FROM

The UX critique: ten competing tap targets, actions mixed with destinations, Listen with no state, faded translations.

PRINCIPLES
  • Tabs are places: Discover, Library, Saved, You.
  • One action row per poem. Play is primary.
  • Every word is tappable. Translations pass 4.5:1.
  • No floating buttons, no badges.
FLOW
  1. Discover today's poem
  2. Read with one action row
  3. Listen as the text follows
  4. Tap a word for its gloss
  5. Save, then share a verse card
  6. Find it again in Saved
Measure (hypothesis): share of sessions that use Listen or tap a word, compared with today's Insights taps.
