You finished Elegy for Sakhr.

The guide would take you to one of these next.

Another woman of the desert

Layla al-Akhyaliyya answered grief with defiance.

ليلى الأخيلية

The mystic's answer

Rabiʿa al-ʿAdawiyya turned loss into love of God.

رابعة العدوية

Same sorrow, 1,300 years on

A modern elegy that would make Al-Khansa nod.
