Khatam · eight directions from B1 A

KHATAM · 01

A1 · REFINED

The original, rebalanced: thinner star, ش sized to sit in the star's optical center.

A2 · ʿAYN

The same star around ع, tying the mark to the Shiعr name.

A3 · SEAL

Khatam literally means seal or signet ring. A solid star with ش cut out, like a stamp.

A4 · SOFTENED

Rounded corners make it friendlier and less like a formal emblem.

A5 · WOVEN

The squares become interlaced bands, the way girih craftsmen build it.

A6 · SPLIT BAYT

The star divided like a verse line, two halves and a caesura.

A7 · PEN STROKE

Drawn as one unclosed line with a reed-pen dot. Handmade, not a symbol.

A8 · STAR DOTS

ش is س plus three dots. Here the dots are three tiny khatams.
