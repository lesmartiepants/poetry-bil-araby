DIRECTION 05 · THE LEARNER

Majlis

A study circle in your pocket. One verse a day, read in layers, explored through roots, recited aloud and kept with spaced review. Poetry as the way into Arabic.

Built from

The critique's missing learner features: tap for meaning, transliteration, meter. Plus the tashkeel the current app already does well.

Principles
  • Layers you switch on and off, not modes.
  • Roots are the map of the language.
  • Speak it, then hear what to fix.
  • Feedback uses icons and words, not color alone.
Flow
  1. Today's verse and due reviews
  2. Layered reader
  3. Root explorer
  4. Recite the line aloud
  5. Pronunciation feedback
  6. Spaced review cards
Open question: speech scoring for classical Arabic needs testing with real reciters before it ships.
