DIRECTION 02 · THE MOTION OBSESSIVE

Ink

Zero chrome. One couplet fills the screen, the voice leads, and the words warm from grey to gold as they are spoken. The poem is the interface.

BUILT FROM

The first designer's one-breath vision, plus the motion obsessive: "a poem about a rose, and nothing moves?"

PRINCIPLES
  • No buttons until the poem ends.
  • Gestures only: hold, tap, swipe up.
  • A gold thread on the edge is the progress bar.
  • Motion follows the recitation, never decoration.
FLOW
  1. Ink drop opens the poem
  2. Voice lights each word
  3. Hold to reveal the English
  4. Tap a word: its root blooms
  5. Swipe up to the next couplet
  6. The poem gathers into one card
Risk: hidden gestures. A one-time coach mark on first launch, and Reduce Motion swaps animation for a static highlight.
