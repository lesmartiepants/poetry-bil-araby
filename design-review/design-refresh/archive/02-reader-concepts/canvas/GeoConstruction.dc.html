Construction · one compass, one rule

GEOMETRY · 03
1

One circle

Everything starts from a single compass sweep. Radius = 1 unit.

2

Eight divisions

Bisect twice to get 8 equal angles, the base of the khatam.

3

Two squares

Join every second point. The overlap is the eight-point star.

4

Petals

Each petal is a rhombus whose tips touch the inner and outer circles.

Why it matters: a mark built from one circle can be redrawn by anyone, at any size, and generates the pattern, ornaments and icon grid from the same unit.
