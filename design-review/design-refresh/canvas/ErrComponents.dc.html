Errors

C20–C22 · COMPONENTS

Three ways to fail gracefully. Pick by how much failed: a part of the screen, an action, or the whole screen.

Inline error · margin noteC20
The guide’s notes didn’t load. Try again

Takes the place of the part that failed (a sheet’s content, the player, a section). An old-gold rule and one sentence in the poem’s italic, with the action underlined. The whole note is one button, min 44 px; mirrored on right-aligned screens. Pressed: rule turns gold, text ivory.

Error toastC21
!Couldn’t save to PhotosSETTINGS

For an action that failed (save, send, sign in, keep). The parchment toast with a pomegranate ring and !, one line and one action. Sits above the dock for 6 s; stays if the action is needed.
